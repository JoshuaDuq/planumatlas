# 0013 Where the arousal nuclei must lie: aseg's Brain-Stem and VentralDC

Status: accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-17, after the first runs and before this rule was applied.

- **Problem.** WP29 fixed two checks before placing the Harvard arousal nuclei on CDMD: the centroids of the dorsal raphe, ventral tegmental area and periaqueductal grey within 3 mm of NextBrain's, and at least 95 % of every nucleus inside aseg's Brain-Stem (label 16). Both methods failed the second. SegmentAAN passed the first (1.23, 0.44 and 1.20 mm), but its ventral tegmental area lay 3.0 % inside Brain-Stem, its pedunculotegmental nuclei 54–65 % and its periaqueductal grey 89.6 %; SynthMorph's VTA lay 28.9 % inside.
- **Why the check was wrong.** The check exists to catch a nucleus placed in the wrong tissue: the cerebellum, a ventricle, white matter or cortex. Measured against Brain-Stem alone it also rejects a nucleus placed correctly, because FreeSurfer's aseg labels the ventral midbrain, where the VTA and the substantia nigra lie, VentralDC (ventral diencephalon), not Brain-Stem. SegmentAAN itself segments within a mask of brainstem, thalamus and diencephalon. The fault is in the envelope, not in the 95 % limit.
- **Decision.** The envelope is aseg's Brain-Stem (16), Left-VentralDC (28) and Right-VentralDC (60). At least 95 % of every one of the 16 sided nuclei must lie in it as placed, and the published nuclei are kept to it. The centroid check, its 3 mm limit, the selection rule and the all-or-nothing outcome are unchanged.
- **Not changed, on purpose.** The ventricles stay outside the envelope: the periaqueductal grey surrounds the aqueduct, and at aseg's 1 mm grid some of it falls in the ventricle label, but counting ventricle as tissue would fit the rule to this result. Publishing only the nuclei that pass is also not allowed.
- **Applied once.** The rule is measured again on the outputs both methods already wrote; neither method is re-run. Whatever the outcome, `docs/features/arousal-nuclei.md` reports both the first and the revised check.
- **Outcome (2026-11-17): not published.** With the envelope, SegmentAAN's ventral tegmental area and pedunculotegmental nuclei lie at least 99 % inside, but its periaqueductal grey lies 90.7 % inside: 9.0 % of it falls in aseg's 4th-Ventricle label, which covers the aqueduct the grey surrounds. SynthMorph still fails both checks (grey 82.7 %, 17.3 % in 4th-Ventricle). As this record decided, ventricle voxels are not counted as tissue, so neither method passes and the layer stays undeclared. Any further change to the check is a new decision, made before it is applied.
