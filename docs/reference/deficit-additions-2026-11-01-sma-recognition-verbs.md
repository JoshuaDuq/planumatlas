## Inclusion decisions

Selection required peer-reviewed primary human patient evidence and complementary evidence from more than one research group. Each profile also had to name a location defensible as an atlas landmark.

- **Supplementary motor area syndrome** is a transient postoperative deficit of initiation, distinct from akinetic mutism and from weakness.
- **Recollection and familiarity deficits** concern a dissociation within recognition memory. It is included with the opposing evidence, because whether the two processes separate anatomically is disputed.
- **Impaired action naming** concerns verbs and is distinct from the noun-naming findings under aphasia.

This is a targeted content review, not a systematic review or independent specialist assessment. Studies from one group are not treated as independent replications.
