## Inclusion decisions

Selection required peer-reviewed primary human patient evidence and complementary evidence from more than one research group. Each profile also had to name a location defensible as an atlas landmark. Cardiac autonomic dysfunction after insular stroke is a medical complication, like the post-stroke urinary retention already profiled. Persistent hiccups is a respiratory reflex disorder distinct from dysphagia and from crossed sensory loss, although all can follow the same lateral medullary infarct. Impaired motor adaptation is distinct from cerebellar ataxia: one study found adaptation impaired with little ataxia, and ataxia with adaptation spared.

This is a targeted content review, not a systematic review or independent specialist assessment. Studies from one centre are not treated as independent replications.

## Source identity and extraction scope

The 12 PMIDs, titles, author lists, issue years and DOIs were fetched with the existing PubMed/Crossref reference-checking code and merged into the reference cache without altering earlier records. No linked correction, retraction or expression of concern was returned. All 12 references use abstract scope. The catalogue now contains 64 full-text and 196 abstract-based reference records.

## Screened evidence not imported

- **Symptomatic palatal tremor.** [Deuschl et al. (1994)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7922465/) describe six acquired cases with inferior olivary MRI change. Goyal et al. (2000, PMID 10871017) has no DOI in PubMed or Crossref, which the catalogue requires. Samuel et al. (2004, PMID 15090471) concerns a progressive degenerative form. One acquired series was judged insufficient.
- **Impaired response inhibition.** Aron et al. (2003, PMID 12536210) has no PubMed abstract to extract from. Picton et al. (2007) and Floden & Stuss (2006) come from one group and place the critical lesion differently. The topic also overlaps the existing executive dysfunction profile.
- **Acquired obsessive-compulsive behaviour.** Berthier et al. (1996, PMID 8757004) has a published erratum whose content could not be read and recorded. That leaves Laplane et al. (1989) as the only series.
- **Callosal disconnection.** Kasow et al. (2000, PMID 11110540) describe atypical radiological cases, not disconnection signs, so the profile remains deferred.

## Verification

Targeted tests fix each finding's coloured regions, keep the left and right cardiac findings' paint separate, keep the prism-adaptation finding uncoloured, and keep lobules I–IV out of the adaptation profile. Each profile is tested to rank first for a distinct search term. A baseline comparison confirmed that every earlier catalogue entry and cached citation record is unchanged. These checks establish integration and provenance, not diagnostic or prognostic performance.
