# Deficit additions: evidence and mapping review

Review date: 31 October 2026. Catalogue revision: `2026-10-31`.

Removed 1 November 2026: pathological laughter and crying; auditory extinction. These profiles coloured no atlas region, so they were taken out of the catalogue with the references no remaining finding cites. The review below is kept as a historical record; its counts describe the catalogue at the time.

Two passes add ten profiles, 16 findings and 26 references to the existing catalogue, bringing it to 80 profiles, 124 findings and 157 references. All new content is available in English and French. The second pass preserves every profile, finding, reference and cached citation record from the first pass.

## Inclusion decisions

Selection required human patient evidence in peer-reviewed primary research, a deficit distinct from the existing profiles, and complementary publications that support or qualify the association. Healthy activation alone, a single isolated case, or an attractive anatomical label was insufficient for adding a profile. Multiple publications do not necessarily represent independent patient samples or direct replications. In particular, published Holmes tremor cases may recur across the clinical series and network literature.

Every new PMID, title, author list, issue year and DOI was retrieved through the existing PubMed/Crossref reference-checking code. No linked correction, retraction or expression of concern was returned for these 26 works. This is a targeted educational-content review, not a systematic review or independent specialist assessment. Methodological limits remain visible in each finding.

## Extraction scope

Seven added references use full-text extraction: Raina, Joutsa, Mackey, the one-page Klos report, Gooch, Kamtchum-Tatuene, and Takahashi. Main prose was consulted for the populations, results and limitations used here. For Raina, the focal right thalamic case is described under clinical–radiologic correlation. For Joutsa, case selection, normative-connectome methods and the discussion distinguish connected nodes from injury. For Mackey, Table 1, the task methods, results, Table 2 and discussion establish laterality, sample size, task specificity and normal standardized working-memory scores. Klos's case prose and caption specify left anterior thalamic haemorrhage and right arm asterixis.

For Gooch, the two duration-specific VLSM Results sections distinguish variability from accuracy and list BA 9 only at 2,000 ms (73 voxels). The abstract/discussion overgeneralize middle frontal involvement, while the detailed Results list precentral BA 6 at both durations despite its subsecond-only description in the abstract. Only the unambiguous BA 44 and duration-qualified BA 9 findings are mapped. The study's imputed extreme scores, uneven coverage, absence of corrected accuracy voxels, and voxelwise versus regional parietal discrepancies constrain interpretation. No timing effect is inferred for untested cerebellar lesions or poorly covered supplementary motor cortex.

For Kamtchum-Tatuene, participant selection, auditory-test definitions, Table 3, the standardized-results section and the pooled anatomical analysis were checked. The latter is not a subtype-specific VLSM. For Takahashi, the main case examination, MRI description, Table 1 and discussion were checked. The paper's retelling of Lee differs from Lee's own abstract in sex and associated symptoms; the catalogue follows the primary Lee abstract for that case and uses Takahashi only for its own directly reported white-matter case. No age, symptom or outcome is silently reconciled across conflicting accounts.

The remaining 19 references retain `abstract` scope. Indexed keywords and captions were also consulted where available; this does not upgrade them to a complete full-text review. No supplemental patient lists, original imaging data, video, or image pixels were independently reinterpreted. No lesion masks or numerical analyses were reproduced. The earlier [full-text review log](full-text-review.md) retains its historical scope. Current extraction counts are 50 full-text and 107 abstract records.

## Verification

Catalogue checks validate translations, searchable names, groups, citation identity, source labels and mappings against each published anatomy. Targeted checks keep connectivity-only findings and insufficiently resolved lesion locations uncoloured, and keep the two thalamic landmarks tied to their specified sides. A baseline comparison checks that all pre-existing profiles, references, findings and cached reference records retain their values. These checks validate content integration and provenance; they do not establish patient-level diagnostic or prognostic performance.

Verification results after both passes: 2,268 unit tests and all 46 deficit-panel browser tests passed; two unit tests were skipped. Type checks and the production build passed. The documentation build succeeded with four unavailable external-inventory warnings and two pre-existing circuit-document navigation warnings; none concerned this review page.
