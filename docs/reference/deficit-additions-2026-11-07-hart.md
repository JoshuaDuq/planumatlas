# Deficit additions: colour knowledge, spatial relations, mania and visual awareness

Review date and catalogue revision: `2026-11-07`.

This pass implements the four priority profiles from the [Hart candidate review](hart-2016-deficit-candidates-2026-11-07.md): object-colour knowledge impairment, impaired spatial-relation judgement, acquired mania and visual anosognosia (Anton syndrome). It adds four bilingual profiles, seven findings and ten references. The catalogue now contains 125 profiles, 200 findings and 316 references. All 121 earlier profiles, 193 findings, 306 references and previous cached citation records retain their values.

The new profiles describe distinct clinical patterns; they are not new names for achromatopsia, line-orientation difficulty, behavioral disinhibition or hemianopia. Individual cases and selected cohorts do not establish population risks or a lesion pattern that is necessary and sufficient in every patient. The two 2020 mania studies share investigators and may share published patients. Literature cases in the Anton reports may also overlap. Counts are not pooled as independent observations.

The existing function-score and function-bridge records were preserved. No healthy-activation map was derived or equated with a clinical syndrome in this pass.

## Citation identities, corrections and extraction scope

All ten identity records were freshly fetched through the existing PubMed/Crossref checking functions before insertion. Titles, author strings, issue years, DOIs and linked correction notices were checked. No retraction or expression-of-concern notice was returned by these checks. Earlier cache records were retained, rather than all being refetched.

The 2021 Anton report has a [2022 erratum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9616237/), DOI `10.1016/j.cccb.2022.100130`, supplying an omitted patient-consent/ethical statement. This is recorded in English and French on the reference; it is not described as a correction to the lesion findings. Its author strings follow the fetched PubMed identity, whose name order differs from the publisher’s presentation. Monai uses the 2024 journal-issue year rather than the 2023 online publication year.

Three references use full-text extraction scope: Stasenko’s clinical and experimental account; Amorapanth’s patient methods, task results and interpretation; and Monai’s lesion, control-selection and disconnection methods/results. The other seven retain abstract scope. Original clinical images, masks, recordings and participant data were not independently reanalysed. Scope describes the extraction source, not independent specialist approval.

OCD and Klüver–Bucy syndrome remain candidates. The unresolved Berthier correction is not imported, and the textbook’s hyperaggressiveness description is not added to the catalogue. The preceding candidate review remains a historical record of the decision before implementation.

The [subsequent continuation](deficit-additions-2026-11-07-hart-further.md) resolves these two candidates using additional primary sources and adds cognitive estimation and Fregoli syndrome. The totals and decisions above describe this first pass.
