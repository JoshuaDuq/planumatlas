# Deficit additions: line orientation, grammar, medullary sensory loss and smoking

Review date and catalogue revision: `2026-11-01`.

All 104 earlier profiles, 163 findings, 235 references and their cached citation records retain their values. The preceding [reward learning, reasoning, multitasking, grasping and vertigo review](deficit-additions-2026-11-01-reward-reasoning-vertigo.md) and earlier linked reviews retain their historical scopes and counts.

## Inclusion decisions

Selection required peer-reviewed primary human patient evidence and complementary evidence from more than one sample. At least one sample had to come from a different research group, and each profile had to name a location defensible as an atlas landmark. Line orientation is a visuospatial perceptual judgement, distinct from constructional apraxia and from visual verticality. Agrammatism and paragrammatism concern grammar in spontaneous speech, distinct from the existing aphasia findings on comprehension, repetition and naming. Crossed sensory loss is the lateral medullary pattern, distinct from the cortical upper-limb somatosensory profile. Disrupted smoking addiction is included as a documented change in motivation after injury, with conflicting evidence stated rather than resolved.

This is a targeted content review, not a systematic review or independent specialist assessment. Studies from the same consortium or patient registry are not treated as independent replications.

## Source identity and extraction scope

The 13 PMIDs, titles, author lists, issue years and DOIs were fetched with the existing PubMed/Crossref reference-checking code and merged into the reference cache without altering earlier records. No linked correction, retraction or expression of concern was returned.

All 13 references use abstract scope. For Naqvi et al. (2007), the main text was consulted only for the group sizes and lesion sides (13 left, 6 right insular cases), which the abstract omits. This does not upgrade it to full-text extraction.

The catalogue now contains 64 full-text and 184 abstract-based reference records.

## Screened evidence not imported

Callosal disconnection was considered again. Giroud & Dumas (1995, PMID 7673948) remains the only consecutive series found, and searches did not return a complementary series, so the profile stays deferred.

Kim (2003, PMID 12805095), with 130 lateral medullary infarcts, was screened for crossed sensory loss but not cited (see the table).

## Verification

Targeted tests fix each new profile's coloured regions and keep each grammar finding's paint separate. They also confirm that the null smoking study is retained as qualifying evidence and that smoking paint stays on insular parcels. Each profile is tested to rank first for a distinct English or French search term. A baseline comparison confirmed that every earlier catalogue entry and cached citation record is unchanged. These checks establish integration and provenance, not diagnostic or prognostic performance.
